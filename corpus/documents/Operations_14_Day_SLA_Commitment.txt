# 14-Day SLA Commitment

## 1. Commitment

Meridian commits to completing standard customer onboarding within 14 calendar days of receiving a complete setup package. The commitment applies to the Professional and Enterprise plans. Starter plan onboarding is self-service and is not covered.

## 2. Start and stop

1. The clock starts when the complete setup package is received: signed contract, admin contact, domain verification and data export specification.
2. The clock pauses while Meridian waits for customer input and resumes when the input arrives.
3. The clock does not pause for customer holidays, only for Meridian-declared freezes.
4. The clock stops when the production tenant is live and the admin receives the handover email.

## 3. Credits

1. If onboarding exceeds 14 days for reasons within Meridian's control, the customer receives a service credit of 5% of the first-year subscription fee.
2. A delay of more than 30 days attracts a 10% credit.
3. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid in cash.
4. Credits are not available where the delay is caused by missing customer input, third-party integration delays outside Meridian's control, or a customer-requested change in scope.

## 4. Reporting

1. Onboarding status is reported weekly to Customer Operations.
2. Any account projected to miss the commitment is escalated to the delivery lead within 48 hours.
3. Monthly performance is published internally with the number of accounts, the median days to live and the credits issued.

## 5. Exceptions

Non-standard onboarding, including custom data migration and on-premises deployment, is scoped separately and is not covered by this commitment. Sales must not promise the 14-day commitment for non-standard onboarding without Customer Operations approval.

## Annex A. Onboarding stages

Onboarding runs through five stages: contract handover, environment provisioning, data import, configuration and acceptance, and go-live handover. Each stage has an owner, a definition of done and a checkpoint in the onboarding tracker. The 14-day clock covers all five stages from the complete setup package.

The package is checked against a checklist at handover from Sales. Missing items are requested in one message listing everything outstanding, and the clock does not start until they arrive. Sales is told that the clock has not started so the customer conversation stays accurate.

Provisioning is automated for standard tenants and takes under a day. Data import depends on the customer's export, and the specification is agreed before the clock starts so the import does not stall halfway.

Acceptance is a scripted walkthrough with the customer's admin covering sign-in, user provisioning, the three most-used workflows and the audit log. Acceptance is confirmed by email, and the handover email starts the support relationship.

Credits are calculated by Customer Operations from the tracker rather than negotiated case by case: five percent from day 15, ten percent from day 31, shown on the next invoice with a reference to this policy.

Monthly reporting counts only accounts where the clock ran, states the median days to live and lists every credited account with its cause.
