# Intern Buddy Program

## 1. Assignment

Every intern is paired with a buddy from the same team for the duration of the internship. The buddy is not the intern's manager and is not responsible for performance assessment.

## 2. Expectations

1. Meet daily for the first week, then twice a week.
2. Review the intern's first deliverable and give written feedback.
3. Introduce the intern to at least five people outside the team.
4. Escalate concerns to the manager rather than handling them alone.

## 3. Duration

Internships run 10 to 12 weeks. The buddy commitment ends on the intern's last day. Buddies receive a recognition payment of INR 10,000 after the internship ends.

## 4. Conversion

Interns may be considered for a full-time role after a strong final review. Conversion decisions follow HR_Compensation_Bands and the standard hiring process; the buddy does not sit on the hiring panel.
