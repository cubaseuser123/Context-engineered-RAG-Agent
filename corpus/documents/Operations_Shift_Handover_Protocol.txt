# Shift Handover Protocol

## 1. Purpose

Support and operations run three shifts. This protocol ensures nothing is lost between them. It applies to the service desk, the data centre operations team and the on-call rotation.

## 2. Overlap

1. Shifts overlap by 15 minutes.
2. The outgoing shift may not leave before the handover is acknowledged.
3. If the incoming person is late, the outgoing person stays until relief arrives or a manager approves a gap in coverage.

## 3. Handover sheet

The handover sheet records: open tickets with priority, systems in a degraded state, changes in the last 24 hours, scheduled maintenance in the next 24 hours, and anything a customer is waiting on. It is written in the shared operations log, not in a personal note.

## 4. Verbal handover

1. Read the sheet together, not separately.
2. Highlight anything that could escalate within the shift.
3. Confirm the acknowledgement in the log with initials and time.
4. Questions are asked during the overlap, not after the outgoing person has left.

## 5. During an active incident

An incident handover follows IT_Incident_Response_Runbook. The incident commander confirms the handover in the incident channel and the scribe records it in the timeline. The new shift does not restart the incident clock.
