# Contract Review Process

## 1. Scope

This process covers every agreement Meridian signs with a third party: customer contracts, vendor agreements, NDAs, data processing agreements, leases and statements of work. Employment contracts are handled by People Operations and are not reviewed here.

## 2. Service levels

1. Standard contract review takes 7 business days from receipt of a complete request.
2. Expedited review takes 48 hours and requires the department head to state the business reason.
3. NDAs on the approved template are reviewed within 1 business day.
4. A request without the counterparty's full draft, the deal summary and the commercial terms is not considered received.

## 3. Request requirements

1. Submit through the legal request system, not by email.
2. Include the counterparty name, the contract value, the term, the data involved and the business owner.
3. Attach the counterparty's draft and any redlines already exchanged.
4. State the deadline and the consequence of missing it.

## 4. Review standards

Legal reviews liability caps, indemnities, termination rights, data protection, payment terms, governing law and intellectual property. Any clause that deviates from the playbook requires a documented approval from the Head of Legal. Unlimited liability, most-favoured-nation pricing and exclusivity are escalation triggers.

## 5. Signature authority

1. Contracts up to INR 5,00,000 may be signed by a Director.
2. Contracts up to INR 50,00,000 require the CFO.
3. Contracts above INR 50,00,000 require the CEO.
4. Delegation must be in writing and is recorded in the authority register.

## 6. Post-signature

1. The executed contract is stored in the contract repository within 2 business days.
2. Finance is notified so payment terms and revenue schedules are set up.
3. Renewal alerts are set 90 days before expiry for contracts with auto-renewal.
4. Amendments follow the same review process as the original contract.

## Annex A. Intake and negotiation

Requests are triaged on arrival. Triage records whether the contract is standard, negotiable or escalated and assigns a reviewer within one business day. Requests without commercial terms are returned with the missing items listed.

The playbook sets fallback positions for liability, indemnity, termination, payment and data clauses. A reviewer may accept a fallback without escalation; anything beyond it needs the Head of Legal. Every accepted deviation is recorded with a one-line reason so the next review of the same counterparty starts from the same position.

Negotiation rounds are logged with the counterparty's outstanding points and the owner. Legal aims for two rounds or fewer on standard agreements, and a third round triggers a call with the business owner to decide whether to walk away.

Execution uses the company e-signature tool. Contracts needing a wet signature, notarisation or an apostille are handled by Legal and the originals couriered to the contract repository. Signatories must hold authority under Section 5 at the value on the signature date, not the value when negotiation began.

Renewals, amendments and statements of work are reviewed as new requests and inherit the original risk assessment unless the commercial terms change. Legal reports monthly on volume, cycle time and expedited requests by department.
