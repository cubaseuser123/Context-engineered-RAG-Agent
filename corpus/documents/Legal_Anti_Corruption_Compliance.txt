# Anti-Corruption Compliance

## 1. Policy statement

Meridian prohibits bribery in any form, in any country. This includes offering, promising, authorising or accepting anything of value to obtain or retain business or an improper advantage. The policy applies to employees, contractors, agents, distributors and anyone acting on Meridian's behalf.

## 2. Gifts and hospitality

1. Gifts to or from business partners must be under INR 5,000 in value.
2. Gifts above that value must be declined or surrendered to People Operations.
3. Cash and cash equivalents are never permitted.
4. Hospitality must be modest, infrequent, recorded in the gifts register and approved by a manager.
5. Nothing may be given to a government official, including meals, travel or event tickets, without Legal's written approval.

## 3. Facilitation payments

Facilitation payments — small payments to speed up a routine government action — are prohibited even where local practice expects them. An employee asked for one must refuse, report it to Legal and document the request.

## 4. Third parties

1. Agents, distributors and consultants are subject to due diligence before engagement and every two years afterwards.
2. Due diligence covers ownership, reputation, government connections and sanctions screening.
3. Contracts must include anti-corruption representations, audit rights and termination for breach.
4. Payments to third parties must be made to the contracting entity in the contract country; payments to offshore accounts require Legal approval.

## 5. Political and charitable contributions

Company political contributions are not permitted. Charitable donations require CEO approval and must not be made at the request of a government official or a customer.

## 6. Red flags

Unusual payment routes, requests for cash, invoices without deliverables, unusually high commissions, and a refusal to sign anti-corruption terms are red flags. Any red flag must be reported to Legal before the transaction proceeds.

## 7. Reporting and consequences

Reports may go to a manager, Legal or the ethics line and may be anonymous. Retaliation is prohibited. Breach of this policy may result in termination and referral to authorities.

## Annex A. Controls and records

The gifts register is maintained by People Operations and records the date, counterparty, description, estimated value, giver or recipient and approver. Entries are reviewed monthly by Legal and annually by the auditor. A gift that cannot be valued is registered at its retail estimate.

Third-party due diligence is tiered. Low-risk engagements use a public-record check. Medium-risk engagements add a beneficial-ownership check and a sanctions screen. High-risk engagements, including any agent paid a success fee or working in a higher-risk jurisdiction, need an independent report before signature and every year afterwards.

Training is mandatory at induction and every two years, with additional sessions for sales, procurement, finance and anyone who meets government officials. Completion is tracked by People Operations and reported to the audit committee.

Contracts with agents and distributors carry audit rights, a right to terminate for breach without penalty and a representation that no improper payment has been made. Payments go only to the contracting entity, in the contract country, against an invoice that matches the contracted services.

Any red flag raised to Legal is logged with the date, the parties and the decision. Legal may suspend payment while it reviews; a suspension is not an accusation and is not described to the counterparty as one.

An annual risk assessment covers each market where Meridian operates, and its conclusions set the due-diligence tier for the following year.
