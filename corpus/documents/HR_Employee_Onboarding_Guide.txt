# Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1. Before day one

People Operations issues the offer letter, collects signed documents and creates the employee record at least five working days before the start date. IT provisions the laptop and accounts from that record. The manager assigns a buddy from the same team, not the hiring manager, and schedules the first-week calendar before the employee starts. A welcome email goes out two days before the start date with the office address, the first-day contact and the documents to bring.

## 2. Day one

1. Badge collection at reception between 09:30 and 10:30.
2. Laptop and access handover with IT, including MFA enrolment.
3. A 30-minute session with People Operations covering payroll, benefits and the code of conduct.
4. Team lunch with the buddy.
5. No meetings after 15:00 so the employee can complete mandatory training.

## 3. The 30-60-90 plan

Every new employee has a written 30-60-90 plan agreed with their manager in week one.

1. **First 30 days — orient.** Complete mandatory training, understand the team's roadmap, ship one small change or complete one analysis, and meet every direct teammate. Success is measured by access, context and a first contribution, not by output volume.
2. **Days 31 to 60 — contribute.** Own a defined workstream, participate in on-call or customer conversations where relevant, and deliver the first independent output. The manager reviews progress at day 45.
3. **Days 61 to 90 — own.** Take full ownership of the workstream, propose one improvement to a team process, and complete the 90-day review with the manager and People Operations. The review decides whether probation is confirmed.

Plans are stored in the HR system and are visible to the employee, the manager and the buddy.

## 4. Mandatory training

All new employees complete security awareness, data protection, anti-corruption and workplace conduct training within 30 days. Engineers additionally complete the incident response module. Training completion is tracked by People Operations; incomplete training after 30 days is escalated to the department head.

## 5. Buddy responsibilities

1. Meet the new employee daily for the first week, then weekly for the first month.
2. Explain team rituals, tools and where to find things.
3. Introduce the new employee to people outside the immediate team.
4. Flag concerns to the manager early.
Buddies receive a INR 10,000 recognition payment after the new employee completes 90 days.

## 6. Probation

Probation is six months for all roles. It may be extended once by up to three months with written notice and a documented improvement plan. Confirmation is recorded by People Operations after the 90-day review and the manager's recommendation. Probation does not change notice periods, which are set by HR_Exit_And_Separation.

## 7. Equipment and access

IT issues a laptop, monitor and headset. Access follows the least-privilege rule in IT_Access_Control_Standard: new joiners receive standard productivity access on day one and role-specific access after the manager's request. Finance systems require an additional approval from the Finance business partner.

## 9. Version history

Version 4.1 shortened day one to end at 15:00 and added the buddy recognition payment. Version 4.0 introduced the written 30-60-90 plan. Version 3.2 extended probation from three to six months. Questions go to People Operations; IT questions go to the service desk.
