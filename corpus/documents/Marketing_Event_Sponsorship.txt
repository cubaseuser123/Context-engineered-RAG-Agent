# Marketing Event Sponsorship

## 1. Sponsorship tiers

Marketing sponsors eight events per year across three tiers: community meetups under INR 1,00,000, regional conferences up to INR 5,00,000, and flagship events above INR 5,00,000. Tier assignment follows total cash and in-kind value, including booth fees and speaking slots.

## 2. Approval

Community sponsorships are approved by the Head of Marketing. Regional events require the CMO. Flagship sponsorships require the CMO and the CFO. Every sponsorship needs a written objective and a follow-up plan before the contract is signed.

## 3. Branding

Booth artwork must follow the brand guidelines and be approved by the brand team two weeks before the event. Giveaways must be under INR 500 per unit and must not be electronics.

## 4. Measurement

Each sponsorship is measured on leads captured, meetings booked and pipeline created within 90 days. Events with no leads for two consecutive years are not renewed.
