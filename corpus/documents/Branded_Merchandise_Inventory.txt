# Branded Merchandise Inventory

## 1. Stock

Marketing holds branded merchandise at the Pune office: t-shirts, hoodies, notebooks, bottles and stickers. Stock levels are counted quarterly and reconciled with purchases.

## 2. Issuing

New joiners receive a welcome kit with a t-shirt and notebook. Merchandise for events is requested through the marketing request form two weeks ahead. Bulk requests above 50 units need the Head of Marketing's approval.

## 3. Budget

Merchandise is charged to the marketing envelope under Finance_Quarterly_Budget_Allocation. Unit cost must stay under INR 500; electronics and gift cards are not permitted.

## 4. Storage

Merchandise is stored in the marketing cupboard on the second floor. Do not store personal items there. Leftover stock at the end of the year is donated, not carried into the next budget year.
