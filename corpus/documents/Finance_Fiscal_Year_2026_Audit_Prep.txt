# FY2026 Audit Preparation

## 1. Scope

This document sets the schedule and evidence requirements for the statutory audit of the financial year ending 31 March 2026. It applies to Finance, Legal, HR and every department that holds evidence the auditor will request.

## 2. Timeline

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Books closed | 10 April 2026 |
| Preliminary trial balance | 15 April 2026 |
| PBC evidence pack due | 22 April 2026 |
| Fieldwork starts | 27 April 2026 |
| Fieldwork ends | 12 June 2026 |
| Draft audit report | 26 June 2026 |
| Board approval of accounts | 15 July 2026 |

The accounting freeze begins on 15 April. After the freeze, journals require the Controller's approval and are reported to the audit committee.

## 3. Evidence (PBC list)

1. Bank reconciliations for all accounts, signed by the preparer and reviewer.
2. Accounts receivable ageing and the bad debt provision working.
3. Inventory count sheets and the count reconciliation.
4. Payroll reconciliation to the statutory filings.
5. Vendor statements for the top 30 vendors by spend.
6. Contract register with the eight largest customer contracts.
7. Capital expenditure register with capitalisation evidence.
8. Related-party transaction schedule, including board declarations.

## 4. Responsibilities

Finance owns the PBC pack. Legal owns the contract register and litigation schedule. HR owns payroll and gratuity workings. Department heads own their expense evidence and must respond to requests within 3 working days. Requests not answered within 5 working days escalate to the CFO.

## 5. Sampling

The auditor selects samples after fieldwork begins. Finance must be able to produce supporting documents for any transaction within one business day. Documents stored only in email are not acceptable evidence; the finance system or the contract repository is the record of truth.

## 6. Segregation of duties

The preparer and the reviewer of any reconciliation must be different people. Where the team is too small, the Controller performs the review and documents it. This is checked by the auditor and any exception must be reported in the management letter.

## 7. Findings

Findings are tracked in a remediation register with an owner and a due date. High-severity findings are reported to the audit committee quarterly until closed. Repeated findings are escalated to the CEO.
