# Employee Social Committee

## 1. Purpose

The social committee organises team events, festival celebrations and the annual offsite. It has 12 members elected each January and a budget of INR 15,00,000 per year, separate from department budgets.

## 2. Events

1. Monthly team socials of up to INR 1,000 per attendee.
2. Two festival celebrations per year.
3. One annual offsite; family members may attend at their own cost.
4. Events must not be scheduled during the finance quarter-end freeze.

## 3. Budget

Spend is approved by the committee chair and reconciled quarterly with Finance. Alcohol may not be purchased with company funds except at the annual offsite, where a cash bar applies.

## 4. Related documents

- Finance_Quarterly_Budget_Allocation for the HR envelope.
- HR_Workplace_Conduct for behaviour at company events.
