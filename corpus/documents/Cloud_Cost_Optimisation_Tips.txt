# Cloud Cost Optimisation Tips

## 1. Right-size first

Most savings come from removing idle capacity, not from discount programmes. Review instance utilisation monthly; anything below 20% CPU for 14 days is a candidate for downsizing or consolidation.

## 2. Storage

Move objects untouched for 90 days to infrequent-access storage. Delete unattached volumes and old snapshots. Bucket lifecycle rules must be set at creation, not added later.

## 3. Databases

Use reserved capacity for steady-state databases and on-demand for spiky workloads. Review read replicas quarterly; a replica serving no reads is pure cost.

## 4. Tagging

Every resource must carry owner, team and environment tags. Untagged resources are reported weekly and deleted after 30 days if no owner claims them.

## 5. Reporting

The cost dashboard is available to every engineering manager. Budget alerts fire at 80% and 100% of the monthly envelope.
