# Facility Access Control

## 1. Badges

1. Every employee receives a photo badge on day one.
2. Badges are personal and must not be lent to anyone, including teammates.
3. Badge-in events are recorded and used for attendance under HR_Return_To_Office_Mandate.
4. A lost badge must be reported to Facilities within one hour; the old badge is deactivated and a replacement is issued for INR 500.
5. Badges are returned on the last working day under HR_Exit_And_Separation.

## 2. Access zones

The Pune campus has four zones: reception (public), general workspace (all employees), restricted labs (named list) and the data centre (named list plus approval). Access to restricted zones requires the department head's approval and is reviewed quarterly. Tailgating through a restricted door is a security violation; hold the door for a colleague only in general workspace.

## 3. Visitors

1. Visitors must be pre-registered by the host at least four hours before arrival.
2. Visitors sign in at reception, show photo identification and wear a visitor badge at all times.
3. Visitors must be escorted in every zone except reception and the meeting rooms on the ground floor.
4. Visitor badges are collected at sign-out.
5. Unregistered visitors are not admitted, even with a known employee's verbal approval.

## 4. Contractors

Contractors receive a contractor badge valid for the engagement period. Contractor access is requested by the sponsoring manager and expires automatically on the engagement end date. Contractor badges do not grant access to restricted labs or the data centre without a separate approval.

## 5. Deliveries and loading bay

Deliveries are received at the loading bay between 09:00 and 17:00. Delivery staff do not enter the workspace. Packages above 20 kg are moved by Facilities, not by the requester.

## 6. Emergency procedures

On a fire alarm, badges are not required to exit; the assembly point is the north car park. Re-entry requires the fire marshal's clearance. During a security lockdown, no one enters or leaves until Facilities confirms the all-clear.
