# Equipment Maintenance

## 1. Scope

This document covers lab instruments, power distribution units, UPS batteries, cooling pumps and the badge readers on restricted doors. It does not cover IT hardware, which is under IT_Change_Management, or the EHRC quarterly cycle, which has its own plan.

## 2. Calibration

1. Lab instruments are calibrated annually by an accredited provider.
2. Calibration certificates are stored with the asset record.
3. An instrument past its calibration date is marked out of service and may not be used for customer work.
4. Reference instruments are calibrated every six months.

## 3. Preventive maintenance

| Asset | Interval | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| UPS batteries | 6 months test, replace at 5 years | Facilities Engineering |
| Cooling pumps | Quarterly inspection | Facilities Engineering |
| Badge readers | Annual firmware check | Facilities |
| Power distribution units | Annual thermographic scan | Facilities Engineering |

## 4. Replacement

Equipment is replaced at the end of its useful life or when the repair cost exceeds 40% of replacement cost. Replacement above INR 10,00,000 is capital expenditure under Finance_Capital_Expenditure. Disposal follows the data-wipe rule in IT_Data_Classification_Standard where the asset stores data.

## 5. Work orders

1. All maintenance is logged as a work order with the asset ID.
2. Emergency work orders are raised within 2 hours of the fault being reported.
3. Planned work is scheduled at least 10 working days ahead and coordinated with Operations_Shift_Handover_Protocol.
4. Maintenance during the finance quarter-end freeze requires the Controller's approval.
