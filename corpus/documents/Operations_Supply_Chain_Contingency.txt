# Supply Chain Contingency Plan

## 1. Scope

This plan covers critical suppliers of hardware, cloud infrastructure and logistics. A supplier is critical when an outage stops customer delivery for more than four hours. The register of critical suppliers is maintained by Operations and reviewed each quarter.

## 2. Single-source risk

1. Every critical component must have a named alternate supplier or a documented reason why none exists.
2. Single-source components are reviewed annually by Operations and Engineering together.
3. A component that becomes single-source mid-year must be reported to the COO within five business days.
4. Alternate suppliers must be able to deliver within 72 hours of activation; suppliers that cannot are listed as partial alternates.

## 3. Failover procedure

1. Declare a supply incident in the operations channel and notify the COO.
2. Contact the primary supplier's account manager and the escalation contact in Appendix C.
3. If the primary cannot commit to a recovery date within four hours, activate the alternate.
4. Update customers through the communications lead if delivery dates slip by more than two business days.
5. Record the actual recovery time and the cost of the failover.

## 4. Inventory buffers

Critical hardware is held at two weeks of forecast demand. Cloud capacity is held at 30% headroom above peak. Buffers may not be consumed for cost reasons without the COO's approval.

## 5. Contracts

Supplier contracts must include service levels, a recovery commitment and termination rights for repeated failure. Contract review follows Legal_Contract_Review_Process. Payment terms follow Finance_Vendor_Payment_Terms.

## 6. Testing

The plan is tested twice a year with a tabletop exercise and once a year with an actual alternate order. Results are reported to the COO and the audit committee.
