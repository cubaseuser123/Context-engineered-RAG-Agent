# Site Visit Protocol

## 1. Notice

1. Customer, vendor and auditor visits must be registered at least 5 business days in advance.
2. Visits involving the data centre or hardware lab need 10 business days and the data centre owner's approval.
3. Emergency visits for incident support are approved by the incident commander and registered retrospectively within one business day.

## 2. Registration

The host registers the visitor name, company, purpose, zones to be visited and arrival time. Facilities issues a visitor badge at reception; the host must be present at sign-in. Visitors under 18 are not permitted in operational zones.

## 3. Safety and PPE

1. Closed-toe shoes are required in the hardware lab.
2. High-visibility vests and hearing protection are required in the power distribution area.
3. Photography is prohibited in the data centre unless approved in writing by the data centre owner.
4. Visitors must follow the fire marshal's instructions during an evacuation.

## 4. Escort and conduct

Visitors are escorted at all times outside reception and ground-floor meeting rooms. Hosts are responsible for their visitors' conduct. Confidential material must be cleared from screens before a visitor enters. Visitors may not connect devices to the corporate network; guest Wi-Fi is available on request.
