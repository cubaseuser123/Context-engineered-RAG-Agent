# Quarterly Budget Allocation

## 1. Cycle

Meridian plans in financial quarters ending June, September, December and March. The annual plan is approved by the board in March and split into quarterly envelopes. Department heads receive their envelope in the first week of the quarter and may commit spend against it from day one.

## 2. FY2026 Q3 envelopes

| Engineering | 420 | 180 | Includes cloud and tooling |

## 3. Reallocation

1. Up to 10% of an envelope may be reallocated between lines within the same department without approval.
2. Reallocation above 10% requires the CFO's approval and must be requested within 10 working days of the quarter start.
3. Reallocation between departments is not permitted after the quarter's second month.
4. Unspent envelope does not carry into the next quarter except for committed purchase orders.

## 4. Contingency

A 10% contingency is held centrally and released by the CFO. Requests for contingency must state the business impact of not funding and the offsetting saving elsewhere. Contingency requests are decided weekly on Thursdays.

## 5. Commitments and accruals

1. A commitment exists when a purchase order is issued.
2. Finance accrues for services received but not invoiced at quarter end.
3. Department heads review their accrual report in the first three working days of the new quarter.
4. Late invoices against a closed quarter are charged to the current quarter unless the accrual was recorded.

## 6. Headcount

Headcount changes require the CFO and the Chief People Officer to sign off. Backfills are approved by the department head within the approved envelope. New roles in Q3 are frozen unless funded by contingency.

## 7. Reporting

Department heads receive a monthly pack with actuals, commitments, forecast and variance. Variance above 8% requires a written explanation to FP&A. The quarterly business review covers every department with a variance above 5%.
