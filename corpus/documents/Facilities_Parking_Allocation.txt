# Parking Allocation

## 1. Allocation

The Pune campus has 60 parking bays: 20 reserved for directors and above, 30 allocated by lottery each quarter, and 10 visitor bays. Employees apply for the lottery through Facilities during the last week of the quarter. Bays are not transferable and are reassigned when unused for 10 consecutive working days.

## 2. Visitor parking

Visitor bays are released by reception on arrival and must be vacated within 30 minutes of the visitor leaving. Contractors do not receive a bay unless the sponsoring manager requests one in writing.

## 3. Two-wheelers

Two-wheeler parking is unrestricted and available on a first-come basis in the east lot. Helmets must not be left on seats in the rain; covered racks are available.

## 4. Rules

Speed limit is 10 km/h on campus. Overnight parking requires Facilities approval. Electric vehicle charging points are available on the ground floor and are limited to four hours per vehicle.
