# Vendor Payment Terms

## 1. Default terms

1. The default payment term for vendor invoices is Net 45 from the invoice date.
2. The invoice date is the date the invoice is issued, not the date it is received.
3. Payment runs happen twice a month, on the 10th and the 25th. An invoice falling due between runs is paid on the next run.
4. Vendors must quote a purchase order number on every invoice; invoices without one are returned unpaid.

## 2. Early payment discount

1. Where a vendor offers a 2% discount for payment within 10 days, Accounts Payable takes the discount if the cash position allows.
2. The discount is calculated on the invoice value excluding taxes.
3. Discounts are recorded as a separate line in the vendor ledger, not netted against the payable.
4. Vendors offering a discount for payment within 5 days are reviewed by the treasury team before acceptance.

## 3. Invoice validation

1. A three-way match is required: purchase order, goods receipt and invoice.
2. Mismatches are resolved with the business owner within 5 working days.
3. Invoices above INR 10,00,000 require a second Finance review.
4. Duplicate invoices are flagged by the vendor ledger and are never paid twice.

## 4. Late payment

1. Interest is payable on late invoices at 1% per month where the delay exceeds 30 days beyond the agreed term.
2. Interest is not payable where the delay is caused by a missing purchase order or a disputed invoice.
3. Persistent late payment is reported to the CFO monthly.

## 5. Small invoices

Invoices below INR 5,00,000 are settled on Net 30 rather than Net 45. This exception exists to reduce the administrative cost of low-value payables and does not require a contract amendment. Where a contract states Net 45 for a vendor whose invoices are consistently below the threshold, Accounts Payable applies Net 30 and records the deviation in the vendor file. Business owners should expect the shorter term when planning cash.

## 6. Foreign currency

Foreign currency invoices are paid in the invoice currency. The exchange rate is the bank rate on the payment date. Vendors may not require payment in a currency other than the contract currency.
