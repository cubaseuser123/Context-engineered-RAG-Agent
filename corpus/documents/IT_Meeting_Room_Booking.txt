# Meeting Room Booking

## 1. Booking

Rooms are booked through the calendar system. Bookings open 14 days ahead and are limited to 4 hours per booking. Recurring bookings are limited to 8 weeks and must be re-confirmed afterwards.

## 2. No-shows

A room not occupied within 10 minutes of the booking start is released automatically. Three no-shows in a month remove booking rights for the following month.

## 3. Room types

Focus rooms seat 2 and have no video equipment. Huddle rooms seat 6 with video. Training rooms seat 24 and require Facilities approval. The boardroom is reserved for customer meetings and executive sessions.

## 4. Video equipment

Report a faulty camera or speaker to the service desk, not to Facilities. Do not unplug the room PC to charge a laptop.

## Annex A. Room usage rules

Bookings are for the booker's own meetings. Booking for a colleague is not allowed, and an event that names another organiser is transferred rather than duplicated. Rooms are not booked for personal use or to hold a desk.

Hybrid meetings use a huddle or training room with video. The organiser joins ten minutes early, checks camera, microphone and screen share, and leaves the room as found. Cables stay in the room; adapters come from reception against a badge deposit.

Cancellations are made as soon as the meeting is dropped so the room returns to the pool. Moving a booking to another room releases the original automatically.

The boardroom is booked through the executive assistant for customer meetings and executive sessions. Internal all-hands may use it when no customer meeting is scheduled, and only with the assistant's confirmation.

Training rooms need Facilities approval because they are often set up the day before. The requester states the layout and any catering, which follows Cafeteria_Catering_Standards.

AV faults go to the service desk with the room number and a description. Facilities does not repair equipment and no one should attempt to fix a projector or unplug the room PC.

Quarterly usage data is reviewed by Facilities. Rooms used below 30% are considered for conversion to focus rooms, and recurring bookings nobody attends are cancelled.
