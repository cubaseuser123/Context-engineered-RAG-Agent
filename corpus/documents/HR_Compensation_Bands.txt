# Compensation Bands

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the salary bands for each job level at Meridian and the rules for positioning employees inside a band. It is confidential and must not be shared outside the company. Managers see the bands for their own team and up to two levels below; People Operations and Finance see all bands. Individuals see their own band in the HR system.

## 2. Bands

All figures are annual base salary in INR lakhs for India-based employees. Singapore and UK bands are produced by applying the location differentials in Section 5 to these figures.

| Level | Title example | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate | 6.0 | 8.5 | 11.0 |
| L2 | Engineer / Analyst | 11.0 | 15.0 | 19.0 |
| L3 | Senior Engineer | 19.0 | 25.0 | 31.0 |
| L4 | Staff Engineer / Manager | 31.0 | 40.0 | 49.0 |
| L5 | Senior Manager | 49.0 | 61.0 | 73.0 |
| L6 | Director | 73.0 | 90.0 | 108.0 |

## 3. Positioning rules

1. New hires are typically positioned between the minimum and the midpoint. Offers above the midpoint require People Operations approval; offers above 90% of the maximum require the Chief People Officer.
2. An employee whose base salary is below the minimum for their level must be brought to the minimum at the next review cycle.
3. An employee above the maximum is not reduced. Their salary is frozen until the band catches up, and they remain eligible for bonus and equity.
4. Employees promoted mid-year are moved to at least the minimum of the new band at promotion, then to the target position at the next review.

## 4. Review cycle

1. Base salary is reviewed once per year, effective 1 April.
2. The cycle budget is set by Finance in the February planning round and published in Finance_Quarterly_Budget_Allocation.
3. Managers submit recommendations in March; calibration happens in the first two weeks of March.
4. Employees hired after 1 January are reviewed in the following cycle unless their offer letter promises an off-cycle review.

## 5. Location differentials

| Location | Differential |
|---|---|
| Pune | 1.00 |
| Bengaluru | 1.12 |
| Singapore | 1.65 |
| London | 1.85 |
| Remote (India) | 0.95 |

Differentials are applied to the band, not to the individual's salary. An employee who relocates is moved to the new band at the next cycle; a move that would reduce salary is capped at a 10% reduction per year.

## 6. Variable pay

1. The target bonus is 10% of base for L1 to L3, 15% for L4 and L5, and 25% for L6.
2. Bonus is paid in April based on the prior calendar year's company and individual performance.
3. Employees must be employed on the payment date to receive a bonus, except where local law requires pro-rating.
4. Sales roles follow the sales compensation plan, which is issued separately and is not governed by this document.

## 7. Equity

Equity grants are approved by the compensation committee. The grant guideline is 0.02% of fully diluted shares for L3, 0.05% for L4, and 0.10% for L5 and above. Vesting is four years with a one-year cliff. Equity is not part of the band and is not considered when testing whether a salary sits inside a band.

## 8. Overtime and allowances

Meridian does not pay overtime for L2 and above. L1 employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day, not a cash payment. Transport allowance for late shifts is covered by the Facilities and Operations guidance, not by this document.
