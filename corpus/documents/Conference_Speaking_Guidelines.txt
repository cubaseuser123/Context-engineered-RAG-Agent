# Conference Speaking Guidelines

## 1. Approval

Employees speaking at an external conference need manager approval and a review by Marketing. Talks that mention the roadmap, customers or financial results need Legal review two weeks before the event.

## 2. Slides

Use the current deck template. Customer names require written consent. Screenshots must not show real customer data. Avoid forward-looking statements about revenue or product dates.

## 3. Costs

Registration and travel follow Finance_Travel_And_Expense_Policy. Speaking fees paid to the employee are permitted only if the company declines them in writing.

## 4. Recording

Recordings may be shared on company channels. Third-party recordings require permission from the organiser before reposting.
