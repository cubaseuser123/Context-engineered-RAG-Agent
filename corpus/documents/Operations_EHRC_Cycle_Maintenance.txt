# EHRC Cycle Maintenance

## 1. Scope

The Environmental Health, Refrigeration and Cooling (EHRC) system serves the Pune data centre and the hardware lab. This plan covers scheduled maintenance, the quarterly cycle, and the rules for notifying occupants.

## 2. Quarterly cycle

1. The EHRC system runs a full maintenance cycle once per quarter.
2. The cycle requires a 4-hour maintenance window.
3. Windows are scheduled on the second Saturday of the first month of the quarter, from 06:00 to 10:00.
4. Ten working days' notice is required for any change to a scheduled window.
5. The window may not coincide with a declared end-of-quarter finance freeze.

## 3. Redundancy during maintenance

One cooling unit remains online during the cycle. The data centre load must be reduced to 60% of normal capacity for the duration; workload migration is coordinated with Reliability Engineering under IT_Change_Management. If the remaining unit fails during the window, the cycle is aborted and the fallback chiller is started.

## 4. Safety

Only certified technicians may open the EHRC panels. Lockout-tagout procedures apply. The lab is evacuated during refrigerant handling. Incident response follows IT_Incident_Response_Runbook if the temperature exceeds 27°C for more than 15 minutes.

## 5. Records

Each cycle produces a maintenance report with readings, parts replaced and any deviations. Reports are retained for 7 years under Legal_Records_Retention_Schedule and are reviewed by the facilities manager.
