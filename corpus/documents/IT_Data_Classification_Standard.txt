# Data Classification Standard

## 1. Purpose

Every piece of information Meridian holds has a classification. The classification determines who may access it, how it must be stored and shared, and how long it is kept. When in doubt, classify higher and ask the data owner; misclassifying downward is a security incident.

## 2. Tiers

| Tier | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Approved for release outside the company | Marketing site copy, published pricing |
| Internal | Routine business information | Team documents, internal wiki, meeting notes |
| Confidential | Sensitive business or personal data | Customer contracts, employee records, financials |
| Restricted | Data whose loss causes severe harm | Payment credentials, health data, source code secrets |

## 3. Handling rules

1. **Public** — no restrictions beyond accuracy review.
2. **Internal** — company accounts only; may be shared with contractors under NDA.
3. **Confidential** — need-to-know; encrypted at rest and in transit; no sharing to personal email or personal cloud storage.
4. **Restricted** — named-individual access; encryption with company-managed keys; export requires the data owner's written approval; no copies on endpoints.

## 4. Retention

| Tier | Retention | Disposal |
|---|---|---|
| Public | 1 year after supersession | Delete |
| Internal | 3 years | Delete |
| Confidential | 5 years | Secure delete with certificate |
| Restricted | 7 years | Secure delete with certificate and audit record |

Where a legal hold applies under Legal_Subpoena_And_Litigation_Hold, retention is suspended and deletion is prohibited until Legal releases the hold in writing.

## 5. Storage and sharing

Approved storage is the company drive, the data warehouse and the CRM. Personal drives, personal email and consumer messaging apps are not approved for Confidential or Restricted data. Sharing outside the company requires a contract and, for Restricted data, a Data Processing Agreement reviewed by Legal. Links to Confidential documents must be restricted to named users, not "anyone with the link".

## 6. Labelling

Documents must carry the tier in the header or the document properties. Emails containing Confidential or Restricted attachments must carry the tier in the subject prefix. Systems that store customer data must tag records with the tier in metadata so retention jobs can act on them.

## 7. Incidents

A suspected exposure of Confidential or Restricted data is a security incident and must be reported within one hour under IT_Incident_Response_Runbook. Do not attempt to delete evidence; preserve logs and notify the security team.
