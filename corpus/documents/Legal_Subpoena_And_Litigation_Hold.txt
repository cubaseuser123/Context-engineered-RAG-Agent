# Subpoena and Litigation Hold

## 1. Immediate steps

1. Anyone who receives a subpoena, court order, regulatory request or formal notice of a dispute must notify Legal within 24 hours.
2. Do not respond to the requester, confirm receipt, or discuss the matter outside Legal.
3. Do not delete, edit or move any related record, including chat messages, emails and tickets.
4. Legal appoints a single point of contact for the matter.

## 2. Litigation hold

1. Legal issues a written hold to the custodians it identifies.
2. Custodians must acknowledge the hold within two business days.
3. The hold suspends every deletion rule in Legal_Records_Retention_Schedule, including automated purge jobs.
4. IT must suspend mailbox and backup deletion for the named custodians until Legal releases the hold.
5. Any loss of data after a hold is issued must be reported to Legal immediately.

## 3. Preservation scope

Preservation covers documents, emails, chat, tickets, code repositories, meeting recordings and device data. Legal defines the date range and custodian list; anything outside the range is not preserved but must not be destroyed if it may become relevant.

## 4. Collection and review

Collection is performed by IT at Legal's direction, with a documented chain of custody. Review is conducted by Legal and, where required, external counsel. Access to collected material is limited to the matter team.

## 5. Confidentiality

The existence of a subpoena is confidential and must not be discussed with customers, vendors or colleagues outside the matter team unless Legal approves. Breach of confidentiality can itself create legal exposure.

## 6. Release

Legal releases the hold in writing when the matter concludes. Normal retention resumes from the release date, and the retention clock is not shortened by the hold period.

## 7. Related documents

- Legal_Records_Retention_Schedule for normal retention.
- IT_Data_Classification_Standard for data handling.
- IT_Acceptable_Use_Policy for communication channels in scope.
- Legal_Data_Privacy_GDPR_Compliance for personal data in collected material.
