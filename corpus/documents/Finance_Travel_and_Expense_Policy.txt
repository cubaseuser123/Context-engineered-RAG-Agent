# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy covers business travel and reimbursable expenses for all employees and contractors travelling on Meridian business. It sets the approval path, the limits and the evidence required. Expenses outside the limits are not reimbursed unless an exception is approved before the spend.

## 2. Approval before booking

1. All domestic travel requires manager approval before booking.
2. International travel requires manager and department head approval.
3. Travel to a country where Meridian has no entity also requires Legal review for immigration and tax.
4. Bookings made without approval are reimbursed only at the discretion of the Finance business partner.
5. Travel must be booked through the corporate travel tool; direct bookings are reimbursed only when the tool cannot serve the route.

## 3. Air and rail

1. Economy class for flights under six hours.
2. Premium economy for flights of six hours or more.
3. Business class only for L6 and above on flights over nine hours, or with medical approval.
4. Rail travel is booked in the class equivalent to the flight entitlement.
5. Changes for personal reasons are not reimbursable.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotel caps are per night, excluding taxes:

| City tier | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru) | INR 6,000 |
| Tier 2 (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai) | INR 4,500 |
| Tier 3 (all other cities) | INR 3,000 |

Role multipliers apply to the cap: L2 and above 1.15, L4 and above 1.20, Director and above 1.33. A Director staying in Mumbai therefore has a cap of INR 8,000 per night. The multiplier applies to the tier cap, not to the actual invoice, and does not stack with any other allowance.

## 5. Per diem

1. Domestic per diem is INR 2,500 per day.
2. International per diem follows the country table published by Finance each January.
3. Per diem covers meals and incidentals. Meals provided by the company or included in a conference fee are deducted at INR 600 per provided meal.
4. Per diem is paid for each night away plus one travel day, not for days worked at the home office.

## 6. Ground transport

1. Airport transfers by taxi or ride-hailing are reimbursable with a receipt.
2. Personal car use is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre, capped at the cost of a train ticket for the same journey.
3. Rental cars require prior approval and are not permitted in cities with reliable ride-hailing.
4. Parking at the airport is reimbursable for trips longer than two nights.

## 7. Evidence and submission

1. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
2. Every claim needs an itemised receipt; card statements alone are not evidence.
3. Claims above INR 25,000 need the department head's approval in addition to the manager's.
4. Duplicate claims are treated as a conduct matter, not an accounting error.

## 8. Non-reimbursable items

Alcohol, personal entertainment, minibar, laundry on trips shorter than four nights, airline lounge access without status, traffic fines, and travel insurance purchased separately where corporate cover applies.
